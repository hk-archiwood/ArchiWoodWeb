    FAQ

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Trial, customization, deployment, and system requirements.

    Is there a trial version?

    We do not currently offer a public trial. For evaluation, we provide video demos, detailed manuals, and remote meetings so you can quickly understand the system.

    Do you offer customization?

    Yes. We can deliver deeper customization such as specific feature modules or special deployment setups.

    Can it produce renderings?

    We offer a third-party real-time rendering system (sold separately). One-click renders, panoramas, and VR — and you can package scenes for clients.

    How is the system deployed?

    Cloud architecture with a simple client install and no bulky local database. Enterprise clients can use dedicated servers (client-paid); individual users share a regional cloud server.

    What are the hardware/software requirements?

    Minimum hardware: 8GB RAM, 2GB dedicated GPU, i5 CPU.

    OS: Design and production clients support Windows 7+ and macOS.

    Software: SketchUp 2017 or later.

    Delivery, returns & refunds

    Delivery: Installer is sent immediately after payment.

    Returns/refunds: Generally non-refundable after sale, except when our fault prevents equipment connection or normal production — then a full refund applies.
